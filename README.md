A 100-word villain monologue for a fantasy game: Vey, keeper of the marshlights along the Drowned Road, greets the hero who came to break his curse — and quietly hands over the weight of forty years of keeping the lamps lit. The tiredness lives in the pauses, the deflections, and the ledger, never in a plain announcement, and the last line lands the sting.
